Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about trauma therapy, insurance, and scheduling.

How do I schedule an initial consultation? +

You can schedule your consultation instantly through our online booking tool. Click on "Book Online" in the navigation bar, choose "Initial 15-Minute Consultation," select a time that fits your schedule, and enter your details. We will call you at your selected time.

Do you accept insurance? +

Thrive Mental Health Solutions operates as an in-network and out-of-network provider.

Insurance I accept: Amerihealth Administrators, Cigna, EAP: Cigna, GTEB, EAP: Amazon Benefits.

Insurance In Progress to be in-network: Aetna & Florida Blue

What is your cancellation policy? +

We maintain a strict 24-hour cancellation policy. If you need to cancel or reschedule an appointment, please notify our office at least 24 hours in advance of your scheduled time. Late cancellations or missed appointments without 24-hour notice will be charged the full session fee.

Are sessions available online or in person? +

We offer both in-person sessions at our Coral Gables office and secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth (online video) sessions for residents of Florida. Telehealth is highly effective, secure, and offers excellent convenience for clients across South Florida.
